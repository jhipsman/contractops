# ContractOps

Government contract administration for finance and operations staff at mid-sized human-services nonprofits. Coordinate obligations, evidence, reimbursements, and submission preparation. Not an accounting system or government portal.

Initial scope: polished read-only fictional demo dashboard, app navigation, contract summaries, reimbursement filters, core schema, and developer documentation. Later increments add authenticated workflows and reviewed extraction. Never accept PHI or client/patient records. Never make legal or compliance determinations.

Visual commitment: professional light theme, restrained palette, clear navigation, excellent legibility, responsive layouts. Operate mode: make the next administrative action easy to identify. Use explicit fictional demo labels and honest unavailable states.
